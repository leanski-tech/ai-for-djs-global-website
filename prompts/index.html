Bookings
Reply to an inquiry in two minutes
You are my booking assistant. Write a warm, professional reply to this event inquiry. Keep it under 150 words, confirm the date is open, restate the details back to them so nothing is misread, quote my [PACKAGE] rate as a starting point, and end with one clear next step. My name is [NAME], I DJ [GENRES] and I am based in [CITY]. Here is the inquiry: [PASTE INQUIRY]
Set design
Build the energy arc for a night
Act as a set programmer. I am playing [LENGTH] at [VENUE TYPE] for a [CROWD DESCRIPTION] crowd, doors [TIME], my slot [SLOT TIME]. Give me an energy arc broken into 30-minute blocks with a target BPM range and mood for each block, three genre pivots that will not lose the room, and the two moments where I should drop the biggest record. Do not name specific songs.
Money
Raise your rate without losing the client
I have DJed [CLIENT]'s event for [YEARS] years at [OLD RATE]. My new rate is [NEW RATE]. Write a short email that raises the rate, names two concrete things they get that they did not get the first year, holds the price without apologising, and keeps the relationship warm. No exclamation marks.
Weddings
Turn a couple's questionnaire into a run sheet
Turn these client answers into a timed run-of-show for a wedding reception: arrival, dinner, first dance, parent dances, cake, open dancing, last song. For each block give the time, who is announced, the mic cues I need to say word for word, and the music mood. Flag anything contradictory or missing that I should confirm before the day. Answers: [PASTE]
Brand
A bio in three lengths
Write my DJ bio in three lengths — 25 words for a booking form, 75 words for a promoter, 200 words for a press kit. Facts: [NAME], [CITY], [YEARS] years, crew or residency [CREW], genres [GENRES], notable rooms or events [LIST], one thing that makes my sets different [DETAIL]. Plain, confident, no industry cliches, no words like "sonic journey".
Content
One gig, a week of posts
From this one gig, give me a week of content: [WHAT HAPPENED]. Include one Reel hook under 8 words, three caption options in my voice (dry, no hype, no emoji unless I say so), a carousel outline of 5 slides, and one story poll question. Say which day each should go out and why.
