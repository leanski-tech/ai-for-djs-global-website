How can AI help me find music for different age groups and cultural backgrounds?

Ask AI for the songs that defined a decade, a region, or a community, then check that list with people who lived it. It saves research time before mixed crowd gigs. Always ask your client what matters most to their family.

The mixed crowd challenge

I play rooms where grandparents, parents, and kids are all at the same party. Weddings in Honolulu often bring local, mainland, and international families together. A school event might have keiki and their parents. Every one of those groups has songs that mean something to them. Finding those songs used to take a lot of asking around.

How AI helps

AI is good at fast research. Ask things like: what were the biggest dance songs in Puerto Rico in the 1980s? What songs do Filipino families usually play at weddings? What are classic Hawaiian songs people dance to at a party? You'll get a starting list in seconds. That list gives you something to bring to the client and the family.

Where it goes wrong

AI can be confident and wrong at the same time. It might mix up regions, name songs that aren't really party songs, or flatten a whole culture into a few famous hits. It can also miss what's sacred or off limits. Some songs are for specific moments and shouldn't be played as background music. Never trust the list alone on cultural music.

Check with real people

Take the AI list to the client and ask: which of these matter to your family? What's missing? Is there anything we should not play? Ask the aunties and uncles if you can. People love being asked, and you'll learn things no tool knows. That conversation is part of why clients trust you.

Build a crate for each crowd

After every gig, save the songs that worked for that crowd in a crate with a clear name. Over time you'll have real tested crates for every kind of room. That's knowledge from experience, and AI only helps you get started.

Quick FAQ

Is it okay to use AI for cultural music research? Yes, as a first step. Always confirm with the client and with people from that community.

What if the client doesn't know what songs to request? Share a short list and ask what feels right. It's easier for people to react than to come up with names from scratch.

How do I handle a request I don't know? Look it up, listen before you play it, and ask the person who requested it when it should go on.
