AI Agents Can Take the Busywork Off Your Plate. Here's How DJs Can Use Them.

You handle the music and the room. Let the agent handle the paperwork.

Most of us love the music and hate the inbox. A new wave of AI agents is built for exactly that gap.

What Is an AI Agent?

  • A chatbot answers a question.
  • An agent takes a goal, breaks it into steps, and uses your connected tools to get it done.
  • You still approve the big moves.

Here's a real assignment: "Research this venue, write three promo captions in my voice, and build a posting schedule." One request, several jobs done.

3. Cue: The Assistant With Its Own Identity

Manus introduced Cue on September 28, 2026. Each agent gets its own email address, phone number, wallet, and computer. You can split a job across agents, one researching venues, one shortlisting, one drafting a presentation. runtimewiresvg

How to Start Without Losing Control

  • Pick one job. Lead follow-up is a good first one.
  • Agent drafts, you approve. Every email, every post.
  • Keep payments off. Add them later, if ever.
  • Feed it your real voice and real services. Generic in, generic out.
  • Check its work every week. Fix what it gets wrong.

The Bottom Line

Clients hire you for your ears, your read of the room, and your years behind the decks. An agent can't do that part.

Let it handle the busywork so you can show up sharper. Learn the tools. Keep your soul.
