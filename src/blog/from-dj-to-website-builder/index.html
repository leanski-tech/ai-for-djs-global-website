From DJ to Website Builder: How Learning to Build Websites Opened a New Income Stream

You can't rely on just one income stream. I didn't set out to become a website builder — but once I learned, it changed everything.

For most of my life, people have known me as a DJ, dancer, teacher, event promoter, and someone who's always been deeply involved in hip-hop culture. Music has taken me around the world, introduced me to incredible people, and given me opportunities I never imagined.

But over the years, I learned something important: you can't rely on just one stream of income.

As DJs, artists, dancers, and creatives, we all have slow seasons. Events get canceled. Budgets get cut. Bookings fluctuate. I started asking myself one simple question: "What skill can I learn that will continue to help people while also creating another source of income?" The answer surprised me.

It Started With My Own Website

Like a lot of people, I wanted a professional website. I looked into hiring someone, but every quote I received was more than I wanted to spend. So instead of paying someone else, I decided to learn. I watched tutorials. I experimented. I made mistakes. I rebuilt pages over and over again.

The more I learned, the more I realized that building websites wasn't nearly as complicated as I once thought. It just required patience and a willingness to keep improving.

Once I finished my own website, people started asking — "Who built your site?" When I told them I did, the next question was always — "Can you build one for me?" That's when everything changed.

Turning a Skill Into a Business

I never set out to become a website builder. It happened naturally. Friends needed websites. DJs needed websites. Dance crews needed websites. Small businesses needed websites. Artists wanted a place to showcase their work.

Before long, I wasn't just building websites — I was helping people create an online presence that represented who they were. Today I've built websites for DJs, organizations, events, brands, and businesses, and every site gets a little better than the last.

Sites I've built include: DJLeanskee.com, EndlessStyles.org, Floorlords.org, BboyElNino.com, 2EZPrinting.com, and AIForDJsGlobal.com.

Why I Enjoy Building Websites

What I enjoy most isn't just designing pages — it's helping people tell their story. A website is often the first impression someone has of your business. If your site looks outdated, or worse, if you don't have one at all, you could be missing opportunities without even realizing it.

I like creating websites that are:

Clean and professional
Mobile-friendly
Easy to navigate
Fast to load
Simple for clients to update
Built to help people get noticed and booked

Whether you're a DJ looking for more gigs, an artist building your brand, or a business wanting to attract new customers, your website should work for you 24 hours a day.

Another Stream of Income

Learning website design has become one of the smartest investments I've ever made. Not just because it generates income, but because it's a skill I can continue to grow for years to come. Instead of waiting for the next event or DJ booking, I can work on websites, help businesses improve their online presence, and continue building something valuable.

It's also shown me that we're never too old to learn something new. I've spent over four decades in hip-hop culture, but I still believe in evolving. Every new skill creates new opportunities.

If You're Ready to Build Your Brand

Whether you're a DJ, dancer, promoter, entrepreneur, or small business owner, having a professional website is one of the best investments you can make. Social media is great — but you don't own those platforms. Your website is your home base. It's where people can learn about you, see your work, contact you, and book your services without distractions.
