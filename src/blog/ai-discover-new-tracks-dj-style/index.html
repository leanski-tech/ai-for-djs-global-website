How can AI help me discover new tracks that fit my style?

AI works best as a research partner for digging. Tell it the artists, eras, labels, and records that define your sound, and ask for related names and deep cuts. Then you listen to every one yourself and keep only what moves you.

Start by describing your sound in plain words

Before you ask for anything, write down what you actually play. Name five records you always come back to, the decades you lean on, and the feeling you want in the room. I come from Boston Hip Hop and breaking, so my list has funk breaks, classic Hip Hop, and the records that get a cypher going. The clearer you are, the better the suggestions get. If you just type "good house music," you'll get the same list everyone else gets.

Ask for paths, not playlists

Instead of asking for a playlist, ask for paths. Who produced this record? Who sampled it? What else came out on that label that year? Which artists did this producer work with early on? Questions like that send you somewhere new, and that's how digging has always worked. AI just helps you see more doors at once.

Check everything with your ears

AI gets things wrong. It will sometimes name a song that doesn't exist, or mix up the artist and the year. So treat every suggestion like a tip from a friend at the record store. Look it up in your record pool, on Bandcamp, or on a streaming service, and listen to it all the way through. If it doesn't fit your set, it doesn't go in the crate. Your taste is still the filter.

Build a habit you can keep

Set aside one session a week. Pick one record you love, ask AI for ten related tracks or artists, and listen to all of them. Save the two or three that hit in a "new digs" crate, and test one at your next gig. Over a few months, that adds up to a deeper crate that still sounds like you.

What AI can't do here

It can't feel a record the way you do, and it doesn't know your crowd. It doesn't know that a certain song hits different in Waikiki than in Dorchester. That part comes from years in real rooms. Use the tool to widen the search, and use your experience to decide.

Quick FAQ

Which AI tool should I use for music discovery? Any good chat tool like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini works for research. What matters most is asking specific questions.

Will AI suggestions make my sets sound like everyone else's? Only if you use them without listening first. Your filter is what keeps your sets yours.

Can AI find unreleased or rare records? Not reliably. For rare records, it's still crate digging, record pools, and talking to other DJs.
