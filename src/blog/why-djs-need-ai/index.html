Why Every Working DJ Needs to Learn AI Right Now

I'm not being dramatic. I've been DJing for decades. I've watched the industry shift from vinyl to CDs, from CD to laptop, from laptop to streaming. Every time the tools changed, some DJs adapted and some didn't. The ones who didn't adapt didn't disappear overnight — they just slowly got passed over.

AI is the same shift. Except it's faster.

Here's what I'm talking about in practical terms. A client emails you asking about your availability, your pricing, and what's included in your packages. Ten years ago, you typed that email yourself. Five years ago, maybe you copied and pasted from a template. Today, ChatGPT can write that response in 15 seconds — personalized, professional, and better than what most DJs send.

That's not replacing you. That's freeing you up to do what you actually do: perform.

The DJs I know who are using AI are doing more gigs, not fewer. They're responding to inquiries faster. They're writing contracts without lawyers. They're generating social content without hiring a marketing person. They're building the business side of the craft without spending all night on the computer.

Meanwhile, the DJs who aren't using AI are still copying and pasting the same booking email from 2019.

The good news: this stuff isn't hard to learn. It doesn't require a tech background. It doesn't cost thousands of dollars. You need a phone, a free ChatGPT account, and about 30 minutes to start seeing results.

That's why I built the AI Playbook for DJs. Not because AI is trendy. Because it works, and because working DJs deserve tools that actually fit their life.

Start today. Even one prompt. See what comes out. You'll be surprised how fast your whole workflow changes.
