The Solution: AI Email Templates

Here's what you need:

1. Booking Inquiry Response Template

Client: "Hey, are you available for a wedding on June 15th?"

Your AI template response (takes 30 seconds to customize):

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for reaching out! June 15th is looking good on my end.

Before I confirm, I'd love to know more about your event:

How many guests are you expecting?
What's the vibe you're going for? (Ceremony, cocktail hour, reception)
Any specific music genres or songs you want?
What time do you need me to start and end?

Once I have those details, I'll send over a quote and we can lock it in.

Looking forward to making your day unforgettable.

— DJ Leanskee

Time to send: 30 seconds (just fill in the name and hit send)
Without template: 15 minutes of writing
Savings per inquiry: 14.5 minutes
5 inquiries/week = 72.5 minutes saved. Per month: 290 minutes. Per year: 63 hours.

2. Proposal/Quote Template

Client wants pricing. Instead of building it from scratch every time:

Hi [Client Name],

Here's what I'm bringing to your [Event Type] on [Date]:

DJ Services: Professional sound system and lighting, [Duration] of music tailored to your crowd, MC/hosting if needed, setup and breakdown included.

Pricing: [Your Rate] for [Duration] / Travel fee: [Amount] / Total: [Amount]

Next Steps: 50% deposit to secure your date. Final details 2 weeks before. Full payment due 1 week before.

Ready to book? Reply with "Let's do it" and I'll send the contract.

— DJ Leanskee

Time to send: 2 minutes. Without template: 30 minutes. Savings per proposal: 28 minutes.

3. Follow-Up Template (After No Response)

Client goes silent. You follow up — not pushy:

Hi [Client Name],

Just checking in on the proposal I sent over. Still interested in booking for [Date]?

If you have questions or want to discuss pricing, I'm here.

If now's not the right time, no worries — just let me know.

— DJ Leanskee

Time to send: 1 minute. Without template: 10 minutes. Savings per follow-up: 9 minutes.

4. Post-Event Thank You Template

After the gig, you send a thank you and ask for a review:

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for having me at your [Event Type] on [Date]. It was amazing to be part of your celebration.

If you have a moment, I'd love a review on [Yelp/Google/GigSalad]. It helps other couples find me.

If you know anyone planning an event, send them my way.

— DJ Leanskee

Time to send: 1 minute. Without template: 10 minutes. Savings: 9 minutes.
