AI Agents for DJs: What They Do, What They Cost, and How to Pick One

Prices checked November 15, 2026. Prices change, check before you pay.

A chatbot answers a question. An agent takes a whole job, breaks it into steps, and works it through with your tools. You still approve the big moves.

This page is only about real agents. The regular AI apps, the ones you chat with and get an answer back, are not on this list. They have their place, but they do not run a job for you. Agents do.

Here are the main ones, what each one does, how it can help a working DJ, what it costs, and how they are different from each other. At the bottom is the most important part: you do not need all of them.

The four kinds of agents

Most of the confusion goes away when you see there are four kinds.

  1. Personal agents. They live with you day to day. You talk to them like a text. They watch your inbox, your calendar and your follow ups. Muse, Dot, Instinct, Claude Duet, Gemini Task, Hark, Cue and Grok Bot sit here.
  2. Whole job agents. You hand them one big job and they hand back finished work: a report, a deck, a spreadsheet. Manus and Genspark sit here.
  3. Own machine agents. Free or open. They run on your own computer or server, you own the whole setup, and you do the setup work. Hermes, OpenClaw, Nod and AutoGPT sit here.
  4. AI employees. You build an agent for one job function, like handling your inbox or screening calls, and it does that job every day. Lindy sits here.

The agents, one by one

Muse - Meta

A personal agent from Meta. You talk to it in its app or in WhatsApp. It connects to your email, calendar and socials, keeps working in the background, and asks your OK before it sends or buys anything.

How it helps a DJ: booking follow ups, lead research, content planning, a daily to do list that stays on top of you.

Cost: free with a usage limit. Power is $20 a month. Maximum is $100 a month.

Watch out: it is new, and US first. Check every permission before you connect an account.

Dot - OpenAI

Dot is a personal agent that lives inside ChatGPT, and Dot is the agent part. Each Dot has its own computer and browser, remembers context, and keeps working between chats, even when you are not there.

How it helps a DJ: venue research, deadline tracking, booking documents, follow up drafts for your review.

Cost: Dots come with ChatGPT Pro, from $100 a month, or with Business Premium plans. They are not part of Plus.

Watch out: that is a lot of money for one agent. Only worth it if you already live in ChatGPT for work.

Instinct

Instinct is a personal agent you just text or call, like texting a person. No new app to learn. It has its own phone number, computer and email, and it can talk to your friends' agents to plan things together. It launched in August 2026 and it is invite only while it grows.

How it helps a DJ: text it from the road. Book the hotel, move the meeting, chase the client who has not answered, all from your messages.

Cost: free while it is in beta. No public price yet.

Watch out: you need an invite, and it can ping you a lot while it learns. Set quiet hours early.

Claude Duet - Anthropic

Claude Duet is Anthropic's personal agent, separate from the regular Claude chat. It runs in the background on its own cloud computer, watches your inbox, calendar and invoices, sends you a morning brief, and checks in when it needs your call on something. It came out October 21, 2026 as a research preview.

How it helps a DJ: a morning brief with your gigs, your unpaid invoices and your follow ups already drafted, waiting for your yes.

Cost: it comes with Claude Pro at $20 a month or Max from $100, sharing that plan's limits. No extra fee during the preview.

Watch out: waitlist only for now, US first. It can misjudge what is urgent, so read the brief before you act on it.

Gemini Task - Google

Gemini Task is Google's personal agent. It started as Gemini Spark in May 2026 and Google renamed it Task. It is the careful one: it works inside Google's apps and checks with you before it acts.

How it helps a DJ: if your life already runs on Gmail, Google Calendar and Drive, this one can watch all of it and run your follow ups without a new app.

Cost: it comes with Google AI Pro at $19.99 a month.

Watch out: personal accounts only for now, and not in every country.

Hark

Hark is a newer personal agent, launched October 6, 2026. Instead of connecting to your apps one by one, it drives real websites in a cloud browser, the way a person would click through them.

How it helps a DJ: web errands. Checking a venue page, filling a form, watching a page for a change, the jobs with no clean app for them.

Cost: free to start. Paid plans are $20 and $100 a month.

Watch out: no connectors yet, US only, and it is the newest name on this page. Start it on small jobs.

Grok Bot - xAI

Grok Bot is xAI's always on agent. It has its own computer, signs into your tools, works a long job through to the end, and checks back when it needs your OK.

How it helps a DJ: long research and lead follow up jobs that run while you are at a gig, and drafts waiting for you when you get home.

Cost: paid plans start at $30 a month, and Grok Bot comes with the paid plans. xAI changed its plans in the fall of 2026, so check the current lineup before you pay.

Watch out: the top tiers cost real money for a solo DJ. One to watch for most of us.

Cue - Manus

Cue is a personal agent app from Manus. Each Cue agent gets its own email, phone number and wallet, and you can split a job across more than one agent.

How it helps a DJ: planning a bigger event, or a corporate pitch that needs more than one agent on it at the same time.

Cost: free early access at launch, by invite.

Watch out: new. Give a wallet only a cap you set yourself.

Manus

Manus is a whole job agent that works on its own cloud computer. You hand it a whole job and it hands back a finished report, deck, spreadsheet or website. Its Wide Research splits a big list across many agents at once.

How it helps a DJ: big research in one pass, like checking 50 venues or sponsors, and building the pitch that goes with it.

Cost: free plan with daily credits. Paid plans run on credits: $20, $40 and $200 a month.

Watch out: big jobs eat credits fast, and there is no cost estimate before a job starts.

Genspark Super Agent

Genspark's Super Agent takes one plain instruction and runs the whole task. It works across nine different AI models and more than 80 tools, and picks the right one for each step. It can build research reports, decks and spreadsheets, and it can even place a real phone call for you.

How it helps a DJ: research a venue or a sponsor and get a finished deck back, or have it call a venue to check a date while you are driving.

Cost: free with daily credits. Plus is $24.99 a month, or $19.99 a month billed yearly. Pro is $249.99 a month.

Watch out: it runs on credits too, and a phone call from an agent is a big step. Test it on small jobs first, and listen to how it handles your name and your business.

Hermes Agent - Nous Research

Hermes is free and open source, and you run it yourself. It remembers you, saves what it learns as reusable skills, talks to you through messaging apps, and runs jobs on a schedule. It can work with many different AI models, not just one company's.

How it helps a DJ: a private booking and content assistant that knows your services, your prices and the questions clients always ask. Good for music library and playlist organization too.

Cost: the agent is free. You pay for the AI model you connect to it, by use.

Watch out: setting it up takes real work. Plan on patience, or a helper.

OpenClaw

OpenClaw is the other big open source agent. It runs on your own machine, and you talk to it from WhatsApp, Telegram and other chat apps. It can read and write files, browse, handle email and calendar, and wake up on a schedule to check things for you.

How it helps a DJ: a morning briefing, inbox clean up, calendar and gig reminders, file chores on your music drive.

Cost: free and open source. You pay for the AI model you connect to it, by use.

Watch out: this one gets deep access to your computer. That is the whole point, and the risk. Use small permissions first, and a spare machine if you can.

Nod - n8n Labs

Nod is a free, open source personal agent that came out November 10, 2026, from n8n Labs. It is a desktop app for Mac and Windows with its own computer and browser in a private sandbox on your machine. You connect the AI model you want, including a ChatGPT plan you already pay for.

How it helps a DJ: errands in your apps and on websites, done on your own computer: download the invoices, cancel the subscription, watch a price, sort the files.

Cost: free. You pay your model provider, or run a free local model.

Watch out: it is a research preview, so expect rough edges. It shows you a receipt for what each task cost, which helps.

AutoGPT

AutoGPT is the agent that started this whole wave back in 2023: give it a goal and it breaks the goal into steps and works them on its own. Today it is a platform where agents, including persistent ones it calls Experts, run on schedules and triggers, on their cloud or on your own server.

How it helps a DJ: repeating background jobs, like watching for new leads or checking the same sites every week, once somebody sets it up right.

Cost: free if you host it yourself. Their cloud plans are $50 and $320 a month.

Watch out: this one is for people who like to tinker, or who have a tech person. Most DJs will be happier with one of the personal agents above.

Lindy

Lindy is where you hire an AI employee instead of building a workflow. You give one agent a whole job function, like running your inbox, screening your calls, or booking your meetings, and it does that job every day, with your rules.

How it helps a DJ: an inbox agent that answers first questions with your prices and your services, holds dates, and books the call, so no inquiry sits unanswered while you are on stage.

Cost: free trial credits to start. Paid plans run about $30 to $50 a month per user, on credits.

Watch out: credits burn faster on calls and research. Watch the first month close.

Pick the one that works for you

Read this part twice.

You do not have to have all of them. You do not need fifteen agents. Most working DJs need one.

Pick the one that fits your business:

  • You want one helper for your whole day: start with Muse. Dot if you already pay for ChatGPT Pro.
  • You want to just text your agent like a person: Instinct, if you can get an invite.
  • You already pay for Claude: Claude Duet, join the waitlist.
  • Your life runs on Google: Gemini Task.
  • You hand off big research jobs: Manus, or Genspark.
  • A job keeps eating your week, like your inbox: Lindy.
  • You are planning something big with a lot of moving parts: Cue.
  • Long jobs while you are out working: Grok Bot.
  • You want to own the whole thing and pay no monthly fee: Hermes, OpenClaw or Nod, if you are willing to do the setup. AutoGPT if you like to tinker.

Start with the free tier. Give it one real job, like following up your last five inquiries. If it saves you time for two weeks straight, then it earns the paid plan.

And keep the rule that keeps you safe: the agent drafts, you approve. Every email, every post, every time. No agent can read a room, pick the next record, or earn a client's trust. That part is still you.
